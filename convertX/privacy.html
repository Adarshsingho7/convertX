ConvertX is a completely client-side application. We do not collect, harvest, or store any personal information on our servers. All conversion history, favorites, and settings are stored locally on your device using your browser's Local Storage.

We use Google AdSense to serve advertisements to help keep this tool free. Please be aware of the following regarding our ad partners:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to ConvertX and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.
